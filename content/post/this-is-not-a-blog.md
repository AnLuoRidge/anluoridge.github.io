It's not a blog. It's just a note sharing site.

I created it because I have a habit of noting down everything, although most of the time I'm too lazy to compile them into articles. Nevertheless, I think some of them is still worth sharing even in a very loose format. This is based on my years of experience of Googling. Sometimes, even several words from the search results may help.

I'm also using the site to improve my note taking. Once they get published, I'm under the social pressure to make them more readable.
